Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Get Out Of Jail Free ("we," "our," or "the app") is a personal-safety and civil-rights documentation tool developed by Christopher Stringer. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Our Promise

We built this app to protect people. That means protecting your data with the same seriousness we protect your evidence. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not share your recordings with anyone unless you explicitly choose to.

Data We Collect

  • Video and audio recordings: Captured when you press the record button. Stored encrypted on your device and streamed to secure cloud storage.
  • GPS location: Captured automatically when recording to timestamp and tag your evidence with coordinates.
  • Timestamp and device info: Date, time, and device identifier recorded with each evidence file for chain-of-custody verification.
  • Incident log entries: Information you voluntarily enter (officer names, badge numbers, what happened, witnesses, injuries). This data is write-once with revision tracking.
  • Emergency contact info: Names and phone numbers of people you designate as trusted contacts. Stored only on your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect browsing data, analytics beyond basic crash reporting, or any data unrelated to the app's core function.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.
  • We do not track your location outside of active recording sessions.

How Data Is Stored

Client-side encryption: Recordings are encrypted on your device before being uploaded to cloud storage. We cannot read your recordings.

Local storage: Incident logs and emergency contacts are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage (iOS Keychain and Secure Enclave where available).

Cloud storage: Encrypted recording segments are uploaded to secure cloud infrastructure (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit). Only you hold the decryption keys.

Data Sharing

We never share your data unless you explicitly choose to:

  • Emergency contacts: When recording starts and you have enabled "Alert on Record," your contacts receive a text with your location. You control who receives this and can disable it at any time.
  • Attorney export: When you tap "Export," you choose how to share your incident report (email, download, etc.). We do not send it automatically.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by court order, but we will challenge overly broad requests whenever legally possible.

Data Retention

  • Recordings and incident logs are retained until you delete them or delete your account.
  • You may mark evidence with "Legal Hold" to prevent accidental deletion during an active case.
  • When you delete data, it is purged from our servers within 30 days.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data at any time within the app.
  • Deletion: You can delete any recording, incident log, or contact at any time.
  • Account deletion: You can delete your entire account and all associated data from Settings. This action is permanent and irreversible.
  • Export: You can export all your data at any time.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud / Cloud Storage: For encrypted backup and sync. Subject to Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Apple Sign-In: If you choose to authenticate with Apple. We receive only your name and email.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social media SDKs.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Recording Consent

Recording laws vary by jurisdiction. The app includes a consent-law guide for all 50 US states and Washington, DC. Users are shown a consent warning before recording begins. Users are responsible for ensuring their recording activities comply with local law. We do not encourage, endorse, or facilitate illegal recording.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including client-side encryption, secure key storage, and encrypted transmission. No security system is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.
