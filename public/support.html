  • Your account email address
  • A description of what you expected vs. what happened
  • The browser and device you're using

Get Out Of Jail Free

For issues with evidence recording, GPS tagging, emergency contacts, or your rights library:

  • Recording not starting: Check that camera and microphone permissions are granted in iOS Settings > Get Out Of Jail Free
  • GPS not accurate: Ensure Location Services is enabled and set to "While Using" or "Always"
  • Emergency contacts not notifying: Verify the phone numbers are correct and include area codes
  • Upload status stuck: The app uploads evidence segments continuously. If signal is lost, uploads resume when you regain connectivity.

If evidence was recorded but appears missing, do NOT delete the app — contact us immediately and we can help recover your data.

Gistly

For issues with voice notes, transcription, summarization, or subscriptions:

  • Transcription not working: Ensure microphone access is granted in iOS Settings
  • Pro subscription not activating: Try restoring purchases in Settings, or sign in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase
  • Cloud sync issues: Ensure you're signed in with the same account on all devices
