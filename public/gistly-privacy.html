Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Gistly is a voice-to-action app developed by Christopher Stringer. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

  • Voice recordings and transcripts: Captured when you record a gist. Stored encrypted on your device and synced to your private Supabase account.
  • Account info: Email address for authentication. Stored in your Supabase account.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not access your recordings beyond what is needed to provide the service.

How Data Is Stored

Recordings and transcripts are stored in your private Supabase account with row-level security. Only you can access your data. Audio is processed on-device when possible; cloud transcription uses encrypted connections.

Subscriptions

Gistly Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not collect payment information directly. Subscription management is handled by Apple at Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data persists until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Account deletion removes all recordings and data permanently.

Children's Privacy

Gistly is not intended for children under 13.
